MRX QR SCANNER

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Package: com.sonu.mrxqrscanner

MRX QR Scanner (developed by Sonu Lal) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles device permissions, advertisements, and user data.

1. Camera & Gallery Permissions

To provide fast on-device barcode and QR scanning, MRX QR Scanner requires minimum device permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively in real-time to scan QR codes and barcodes. Frames are processed 100% offline on your device using on-device ML Kit detection. No photos or videos are ever uploaded, recorded, or transmitted to any external server.
  • Gallery / Photo Selection: When you pick an image from your gallery to scan a QR code, the photo is read locally on your device. Your photos never leave your device.

2. Local Offline Data Storage

Your scan history is stored locally in your device's private SQLite database so you can view it offline. We do not upload, sync, or sell your scan history. You can delete individual scans or clear your entire history at any time from within the app Settings.

3. Advertisements & Google AdMob

This app displays banner advertisements and video ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers and technical data to display, personalize, and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Data collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Approximate coarse location & IP address
  • Ad impression and interaction metrics

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Go to Google > All services > Ads (or Ad Privacy).
  • Tap Delete advertising ID or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children's Privacy

MRX QR Scanner is a general-utility application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with the effective date revised.
